These terms cover the HSF AI Founding 100 memberships (Founder Pro and Founder Elite) sold by {legal_entity} ("HSF AI", "we"). They add to the terms that apply to every HSF AI account. Where the two differ for a founding membership, these terms apply.

## 1. Program eligibility and membership limits
- Founding memberships are limited to 100 in total: 75 Founder Pro and 25 Founder Elite. When a tier is sold out, it is not offered again.
- You need an HSF AI account in good standing. Each account can hold one founding membership.
- If your account has an active monthly or yearly HSF subscription, cancel it before you buy. A founding membership replaces the subscription; it is not added on top of it.
- A seat is held for you while you complete checkout. If checkout isn't finished in time, the seat is released.

## 2. One-time payment structure
- Founder Pro: one payment of $499 USD.
- Founder Elite: one payment of $999 USD.
- There are no recurring membership charges, and the membership never renews automatically. We will not charge you again for the same membership.
- Prices are set by HSF AI at checkout. Any taxes required by law are shown before you pay.
- Payments are processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

## 3. Five-year membership duration
- Your membership lasts five calendar years from the moment it is activated. For example, a membership activated at 3:00 pm UTC on 2 November 2026 ends at 3:00 pm UTC on 2 November 2031. A membership activated on 29 February ends on 28 February five years later.
- The end date is shown on your account page.
- When the five years end, membership access stops. You can keep using HSF AI on the free Starter plan or choose any plan offered at that time.

## 4. Membership activation
- Your membership begins only after the payment provider confirms your payment. A started or abandoned checkout does not create a membership.
- Founder numbers (#001 to #100) are given in the order payments are confirmed, across both tiers.
- If a payment arrives after your seat hold expired and the tier has since sold out, no membership is created and the payment is refunded in full.

## 5. Included features and exclusions
- Founder Pro includes the features of the HSF Pro plan, as offered during your membership.
- Founder Elite includes the features of the HSF Premium plan, as offered during your membership.
- Features may be improved, changed or replaced during the five years. We will not reduce your membership to fewer features than the plan it is tied to offers paying subscribers at the same time.
- Premium third-party market data is not included unless we say in writing that it is.
- Brokerage services, trade execution and investment advice are not included.
- Features we describe as planned are not part of what you are buying today. They will be marked "planned" until they are available, and we do not promise when, or whether, they will ship.

## 6. Founder Elite API access limitations
- Personal API access is a planned Founder Elite feature. It is not available today.
- If it is released, it will be for your own personal, non-commercial use, with usage limits, and may not be resold, shared or used to redistribute HSF AI data.
- We may suspend API credentials that are misused, shared or that put the service at risk.

## 8. Membership cancellation

- If you delete your HSF AI account, your membership ends. We keep the payment records the law requires us to keep.

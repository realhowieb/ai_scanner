# HSF Landing Page Product Copy

## Hero

**Headline:** Market intelligence built to find what deserves attention.

**Subheadline:** HSF scans the broader U.S. market, prioritizes developing and
active setups, and explains the signals, cautions, and changes behind each name.

**CTA:** Request private-beta access

## Market Brief

**Headline:** Start with what matters now.

See market context, current opportunities, meaningful changes, and watched-stock
matches in one focused brief.

## Scanner

**Headline:** Scan the market. Find the setups.

Move from a broad market universe to ranked opportunities with price context,
setup status, technical signals, and existing model intelligence.

## PreBreakout

**Headline:** Find developing setups earlier.

PreBreakout ranks current scanner candidates with the existing trained model so
you can investigate potential setups before they become obvious.

## Day Trader

**Headline:** See intraday structure in context.

Monitor price action alongside VWAP, relative volume, ADX, SuperTrend, EWO, and
HSF's deterministic setup summary.

## Intelligence

**Headline:** Know why a ticker matters.

One canonical view connects direction, lifecycle, Opportunity Score,
PreBreakout, ML probability, Alert Priority, supporting evidence, and cautions.

## Watchlist

**Headline:** Your market, prioritized.

Track the names you care about and see which are strengthening, fading, or need
attention as HSF intelligence changes.

## Research and trust

HSF preserves point-in-time observations and follows setup evolution over time.
Its intelligence is descriptive and research evidence is still accumulating;
HSF does not promise returns or present historical observations as trading advice.

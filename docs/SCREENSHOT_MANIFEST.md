# HSF Screenshot Manifest

## 1. Hero — Scanner / HSF Opportunities

- Purpose: prove broad discovery becomes an understandable ranked shortlist.
- Focal point: ranked opportunity table plus one expanded setup explanation.
- Ideal conditions: healthy regular-session scan with varied statuses and models.
- Viewport: 1600 x 1000.
- Headline: **Scan the market. Find the setups.**
- Caption: HSF prioritizes current opportunities and keeps the evidence visible.

## 2. Stock Intelligence

- Purpose: prove HSF explains a name rather than emitting an opaque score.
- Focal point: separate scores, lifecycle, confirmations, and cautions.
- Ideal conditions: a ranked ticker with multiple confirming factors and at
  least one honest caution or unknown field.
- Viewport: 1440 x 900.
- Headline: **Know why a ticker matters.**
- Caption: Current state, model context, evidence, and caution in one view.

## 3. Market Brief

- Purpose: prove HSF provides a daily decision hierarchy.
- Focal point: regime/context and strongest opportunities.
- Ideal conditions: fresh healthy scan during or shortly after market hours.
- Viewport: 1600 x 1000.
- Headline: **Start with what matters now.**
- Caption: A focused view of market context and changing opportunities.

## 4. Day Trader

- Purpose: prove professional intraday signal depth.
- Focal point: compact technical table with Direction, ADX, VWAP, RVOL,
  SuperTrend, and EWO populated.
- Ideal conditions: active session and symbols with cached daily history.
- Viewport: 1920 x 1080.
- Headline: **See intraday structure in context.**
- Caption: Live price, participation, trend, and momentum in one monitor.

## 5. My Watchlist

- Purpose: prove HSF becomes personal and change-aware.
- Focal point: attention summary and strengthening/fading cards.
- Ideal conditions: a real list with several recent intelligence changes.
- Viewport: 1440 x 900.
- Headline: **Your market, prioritized.**
- Caption: Meaningful changes across the names you already care about.

## Screenshots to avoid or replace

- Replace any hero image dominated by scanner filters or a raw 20-column table
  with Manifest #1.
- Replace generic ticker/watchlist tables with the Attention view in Manifest #5.
- Do not use model training panels, admin diagnostics, billing, empty Alerts,
  sparse Historical Replay sessions, login screens, or tables full of `—`.
- Do not capture stale data without the visible stale/freshness label.
- Do not use a PreBreakout capture where all visible probabilities appear equal;
  use Scanner or Stock Intelligence instead.
